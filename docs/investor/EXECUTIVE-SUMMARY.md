### 5. Competitive Positioning Highlights

**Direct Competitors** (Page 3):
- **NONE** *(yet)*
- No one is doing AI governance for e-commerce specifically
- **12-18 month head start**

**Indirect Competitors** (Pages 4-12):
- **LLM Obs Tools** (LangSmith, Vellum): Developer-focused, no policy enforcement
- **Workflow Automation** (Zapier, Shopify Flow): Generic automation, not AI-specific
- **Platform AI** (Shopify Magic, Zendesk AI): Platform-locked, no cross-vendor
- **GRC Tools** (Vanta, Drata): Security posture, not operational governance

**Competitive Moats** (Pages 13-14):
1. **Schema moat**: Nobo Agent Standard adoption → network effects
2. **Integration depth**: Shopify/Zendesk/HubSpot integrations
3. **Insurance data**: 18-24 months of loss data (no one else has this)
4. **Agency distribution**: Once agencies deploy to 50 clients, high switching cost

**Competitive Threats** (Pages 15-18):
- **Shopify builds this** (Medium-High risk, 60% probability)
  - Mitigation: Expand beyond Shopify, add insurance, lock in agencies
- **Zapier adds AI governance** (Low-Medium risk, 30% probability)
  - Mitigation: First-mover advantage, vertical depth, insurance
- **LLM ops tools add policy** (Medium risk, 40% probability)
  - Mitigation: Different buyer (ops vs. dev), e-commerce expertise
- **Enterprise security expands** (Low risk, 20% probability)
  - Mitigation: SMB focus, operational use case

**Win Rates** (Page 19):
- Year 1: 75% (vs. manual review, Shopify Flow, in-house)
- Year 2-3: 65% (harder as competition emerges)
