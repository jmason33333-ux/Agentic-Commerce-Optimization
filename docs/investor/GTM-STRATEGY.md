# Nobo Go-To-Market Strategy
## Governed AI for E-commerce

**Version**: 1.0
**Focus**: Agency-First, Multi-Channel Expansion

---

## 🎯 Strategic Overview

### Core GTM Thesis

**"We don't sell to AI vendors. We sell to the people who deploy AI."**

**Primary customers are**:
1. **Shopify agencies** (10-100 client stores each)
2. **Shopify Plus merchants** (in-house teams)
3. **AI tool vendors** (integration partners, not primary revenue)

**Why this works**:
- Agencies are motivated to upsell "governed AI" to clients
- One agency = 10-50 merchant workspaces (multiplier effect)
- Merchants trust their agencies more than new vendors
- We capture value at point of deployment, not development

## 🚀 Phase 1: Agency-First (Months 0-12)

### Target Customer Profile

**Ideal Customer Profile (ICP): Shopify Agency**

**Firmographics:**
- 5-50 employees
- Managing 10-100+ client stores
- $1-10M annual revenue
- Offers "Shopify Plus services" (not basic theme shops)

**Technographics:**
- Already using: Shopify Partner account, AI tools (catalog, support, inventory)
- Pain points:
  - Clients want AI automation but are scared
  - Can't offer "governed AI" as premium service
  - No unified view of AI actions across clients
  - Hard to audit/report what AI did

**Buyer Persona:**

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Title** | Agency owner, Head of Client Services, Technical Director |
| **Goals** | - Upsell premium services<br>- Differentiate from competitors<br>- Reduce manual client work |
| **Challenges** | - Clients skeptical of AI<br>- Hard to prove ROI/safety of AI<br>- Tools are siloed |
| **Budget** | $500-2K/mo per tool/client workspace |

**Target**: 10 agencies in first 6 months

**1. LinkedIn Outreach**

**2. Warm Intro Network**
- Leverage existing 50+ ACP customers
- Ask: "Which agencies do you work with that are interested in AI?"

**3. Shopify Partner Slack/Discord**

  - Email 1: "The AI governance gap in e-commerce"

### Retention Strategy

**Month 1-3: High-Touch**
- Weekly check-ins
- Ensure they're using approval queue
- Surface insights: "You've governed 150 AI actions this month"

**Month 4-6: Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs)**
- Show value: "AI actions governed, bad actions caught, time saved"
- Expansion opportunities: "Add more workspaces? Enable stop-loss?"

**Month 7-12: Upsell & Expand**
- Identify power users for case studies
- Upsell enterprise features (RBAC, SSO, custom policies)
- Expansion: Agencies naturally add more client stores

**Churn Prevention:**
- At-risk signal: No AI actions in 30 days → reach out
- Churn survey: If they cancel, ask why
- Win-back campaign: 3 months after churn, offer 50% off to return
