## 🚨 Risk Factors

### Revenue Risks

**1. Agency Channel Concentration**
- **Risk**: 70% of revenue from agencies; if this channel doesn't scale, we miss targets
- **Mitigation**: Build direct merchant + PLG motion in parallel

**2. Shopify Vertical Dependence**
- **Risk**: 100% of Year 1 revenue is Shopify-dependent
- **Mitigation**: Expand to support/CX in Year 2, CRM in Year 3

**3. Insurance Regulatory Complexity**
- **Risk**: Stop-loss product may face regulatory hurdles
- **Mitigation**: Start with self-insurance, partner with licensed underwriter later

### Cost Risks

**1. Infrastructure Scaling**
- **Risk**: Per-action costs could be higher than modeled if Shopify API usage is expensive
- **Mitigation**: Batch API calls, cache aggressively, negotiate volume pricing

**2. Customer Acquisition**
- **Risk**: CAC could be 2x higher if agency sales take longer
- **Mitigation**: Focus on ACP customer upgrades first (lower CAC)

**3. Support Burden**
- **Risk**: Agencies may need more white-glove support than budgeted
- **Mitigation**: Build self-serve tools, comprehensive docs, community

## 📋 Appendix: Detailed Assumptions

### Customer Acquisition Assumptions

**Agency Sales Cycle:**
- Outreach → Demo → POC → Close: 45 days avg
- Close rate: 20% (5 demos → 1 customer)
- Sales time per customer: 10 hours (founder-led)
- Onboarding time: 8 hours (technical + training)

**Merchant Sales Cycle:**
- PLG: Sign-up → Activation → Payment: 14 days
- Agency referral: Instant (agency deploys)
- Close rate (PLG): 30%

**Churn Assumptions:**
- Agency monthly churn: 1.5% (18% annually)
- Merchant monthly churn: 3% (36% annually)
- Reasons for churn: budget cuts, switching to in-house, feature gaps

**Expansion Assumptions:**
- Agencies expand workspaces: +30% over 48 months
- Merchants upgrade plans: +10% upgrade to Enterprise within 12 months
- Net revenue retention: 110% (expansion > churn)
